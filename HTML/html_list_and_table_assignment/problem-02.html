Chef's Recommended Dining Experience

Follow our recommended order for a complete dining experience:

  1. Start with Paneer Tikka or Spring Rolls.
  2. Choose Butter Paneer or Chicken Biryani as the main course.
  3. Enjoy Gulab Jamun or Chocolate Brownie for dessert.
  4. Finish your meal with Cold Coffee or Fresh Lime Soda.
