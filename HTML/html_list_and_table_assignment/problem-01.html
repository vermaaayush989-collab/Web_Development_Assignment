GLA University Greater Noida

Welcome to the University Admission Handbook. This page provides important information about the admission process, eligibility, documents and fees.

Admission Overview

GLA University provides various undergraduate and postgraduate courses for students who want to build their academic and professional careers.

Students should carefully check the eligibility requirements and complete the application process before the deadline.

Eligibility

Students must satisfy the required academic qualifications for the selected course.

Students must submit valid academic documents during the admission process.

The minimum eligibility may be different for different courses.

Application Process

Follow these steps to complete the admission process:

  1. Visit the official admission website.
  2. Register using your email and mobile number.
  3. Fill in the application form.
  4. Upload the required documents.
  5. Pay the application fee.
  6. Submit the application form.
  7. Wait for the admission decision.

Application Deadline: 30 June 2026

Required Documents

The following documents may be required during admission:

  • 10th Marksheet
  • 12th Marksheet
  • Transfer Certificate
  • Migration Certificate
  • Government Identity Proof
  • Passport Size Photograph
  • Category Certificate, if applicable
  • Other required academic documents

Important Terms

Eligibility
The minimum academic requirements required to apply for a particular course.
Merit List
A list of students prepared according to their academic performance or admission criteria.
Counselling
A process in which students receive guidance about courses and admission.
Scholarship
Financial support provided to eligible students according to specified criteria.
Registration
The process of submitting student information for admission.
