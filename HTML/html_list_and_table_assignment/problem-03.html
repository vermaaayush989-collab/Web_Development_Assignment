Steps Before a Practical Class

  1. Check the practical schedule.
  2. Read the practical instructions.
  3. Bring the required notebook and materials.
  4. Reach the laboratory on time.
  5. Complete the practical work.
  6. Submit the practical record if required.

Classroom Rules

  • Students must attend classes regularly.
  • Students should reach the classroom on time.
  • Mobile phones should be used only when permitted.
  • Students should maintain cleanliness.
  • Students should respect teachers and classmates.
  • Practical equipment should be handled carefully.

Important Academic Notice: Maintain regular attendance and complete assignments on time.

Academic Terms

Lecture
A teaching session in which theoretical concepts are explained by the teacher.
Lab
A practical session where students perform experiments or write programs.
Tutorial
A session focused on practice, discussion and solving academic problems.
Internal Assessment
Assessment conducted during the semester through tests, assignments or other academic activities.
Practical
A hands-on academic activity used to apply theoretical knowledge.
