Financial Education Topics

Saving Money

Saving money regularly can help you prepare for future expenses and emergencies.

A simple way to start is to save a fixed amount from your monthly income.

Bank Accounts

Bank accounts can be used to store money, receive payments and make transactions.

Loans and Interest

A loan is money borrowed from a financial institution that is usually repaid with interest.

Always understand the interest rate, charges and repayment period before taking a loan.

Important Notice

This website provides educational information only. It is not personal financial or investment advice.

Financial Tips

  • Create a monthly budget.
  • Save money regularly.
  • Maintain an emergency fund.
  • Understand loan interest rates.
  • Pay bills on time.
  • Keep your banking information secure.
  • Compare financial products before choosing one.
  • Read terms and conditions carefully.

Steps to Open a Bank Account

  1. Choose a suitable bank.
  2. Select the type of account.
  3. Fill out the account opening form.
  4. Submit the required documents.
  5. Complete the verification process.
  6. Receive your account details.

Important Financial Terms

Interest Rate
The percentage charged or earned on an amount of money.
EMI
Equated Monthly Instalment paid regularly towards a loan.
Principal
The original amount of money borrowed or invested.
Tenure
The period for which a loan or financial product is maintained.
